# Privacy

Christian's Google Meet Enhancements doesn't collect, store or send any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers: everything it does happens in your own browser.

It only runs on meet.google.com. There it reads the page address to tell whether you're in a meeting, asks Chrome to mute or unmute that one tab when you press its button, and presses Meet's own microphone and camera buttons to switch them off before you join. It never reads what's said or shown in a meeting, and it doesn't keep anything once the page closes.

Nothing it sees leaves your computer, and none of it is shared with anyone.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/christianjbrown/chrome-extension-google-meet-enhancements/issues.
